## Full description (4000 characters max)

This one is about 2,050 characters:

```
VODO HRMS is the official HR app for GlobalSpace employees. Sign in once with your employee code and manage your everyday HR tasks from your phone — no need to open a laptop.

ATTENDANCE
• See your month at a glance: present, WFH, half day, leave, holidays and absences, colour-coded day by day.
• Check in/out times and working hours for every day.
• Missed a punch or wrong time? Request a regularization in a few taps.
• On approved Work From Home days, clock in and clock out right from the app.

LEAVE & WORK FROM HOME
• Live leave balances for every leave type.
• Apply for full-day or half-day leave — the app shows exactly how many working days will be used (weekly offs and holidays are not counted).
• Attach a medical certificate or document using your camera or files.
• Request Work From Home in advance and track approval.
• Rejected or sent back? See the approver's remarks and reapply with one tap.

EXPENSES
• Submit expense claims with multiple items and photograph each bill.
• Track claims from submission to approval and payment.
• Download your monthly expense statement — every expense with all bills in one PDF, ready to share.

SALARY & BENEFITS
• View salary slips with earnings, deductions, loss of pay and net pay.
• Download payslips as PDF.
• Request a loan or salary advance and track recovery.

HOLIDAYS & POLICIES
• Company holiday calendar; claim your optional holidays.
• Read and download HR policy documents.

FOR MANAGERS
• Approve, reject or send back leave, WFH, regularization, expense and loan requests on the go.
• My Team: who's in the office, on WFH, on leave or absent today — and each team member's attendance month.
• See your team's leave calendar and requests.

STAY IN THE LOOP
• Instant notifications for approvals, request updates, payslips and announcements.
• Company-wide birthdays and work anniversaries on your home screen.

SECURE BY DESIGN
• One-time sign-in, stored securely on your phone; sign out anytime.
• All data is encrypted in transit; only you, your managers and authorised HR can see your information.
• HR can sign out a lost phone remotely.

VODO HRMS is for GlobalSpace employees only. Your account is created by GlobalSpace HR — contact HR if you need help signing in.
```
